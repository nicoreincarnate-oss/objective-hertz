## 1. Brand Name and Meaning

**Athena Studios**

Athena is the Greek goddess of wisdom, craft, and strategy. Not brute force — precision. Not extravagance — excellence. She built things that lasted.

Studios signals a creative workspace. Not a faceless corporation, not a solo freelancer scrambling for gigs. A studio: skilled people, intentional process, consistent craft.

Together: wisdom applied through craft. That is what every client gets.

## 2. Tagline Options

### Option A — Craft-Focused
**"Custom websites. Honest prices."**

Rationale: Addresses the two biggest fears local business owners have — paying for a template that looks generic, and getting a surprise invoice. Pairs the quality promise with the transparency promise in five words.

### Option B — Results-Focused
**"Your business online. Your customers calling."**

Rationale: Leads with outcomes, not deliverables. A dentist does not want a website — she wants patients. A plumber does not want a URL — he wants calls. This tagline says what the client actually gets.

### Option C — Bold / Differentiating
**"Professional websites shouldn't cost $5,000."**

Rationale: Opens with a market-disrupting truth. Polarizes immediately — every local business owner who has ever gotten a quote from an agency will feel this. Highest attention-grabbing potential for ads and outreach.

**Recommended primary:** Option B for the website and proposals.
**Recommended secondary:** Option C for ad headlines and outreach subject lines.
**Option A** as a persistent tagline beneath the logo — short, always true.

## 3. Brand Story

Most local businesses are invisible online. Not because they are bad at what they do — they are usually excellent. They just never had the time or the budget to fix it.

Professional web design has a pricing problem. Agencies charge $3,000 to $10,000 for a website because their overhead demands it. Freelancers charge less but deliver inconsistently and go dark mid-project. DIY builders like Wix and Squarespace hand you a template and call it custom.

Local businesses — the dentist on the corner, the plumber who has been in town for twenty years, the restaurant with the best food nobody outside the neighborhood knows about — deserve better than that.

Athena Studios was built to close that gap.

We built a studio from the ground up with one rule: every client gets a custom, professional website at a price that makes sense for a small business. Not a template with a logo swapped in. Not a $299 package that takes four months. A real website, built for their specific business, live in days.

The result: a $299 five-page website that competes with $5,000 agency work. A $149 landing page that converts. A hosting plan that keeps everything running for $52 a month.

We work with businesses worldwide, from a dental clinic in Toronto to a plumbing company in Melbourne to a restaurant in Mexico City. The problem is universal. So is our answer.

## 4. Brand Voice

### Core Personality

Athena Studios sounds like a knowledgeable friend who respects your time. We give you the straight answer before we explain it. We tell you what something costs before you have to ask. We talk about your business, not ours.

We do not use filler. We do not hedge. We do not impress you with jargon. We tell you what you need to know to make a good decision.

### We Are / We Are Not

| We Are | We Are Not |
|---|---|
| Direct | Blunt |
| Affordable | Cheap |
| Confident | Arrogant |
| Professional | Corporate |
| Warm | Pushy |
| Transparent | Salesy |
| Specific | Buzzword-heavy |
| Results-focused | Feature-obsessed |
| Efficient | Rushed |
| Knowledgeable | Condescending |

### Words We Use

- Custom, not template
- Professional, not premium
- Affordable, not cheap or budget
- Transparent pricing, not competitive pricing
- Live in days, not fast or quick turnaround
- Local business, not small business (local has pride; small feels diminutive)
- Craft, not work
- Build, not create

### Words We Never Use

- Innovative, Solutions, Leverage, Seamless, Best-in-class
- Passionate, Synergy, Cutting-edge, Revolutionary
- AI-powered (never mention AI in client-facing content)
- Game-changer, Disruptive, Next-level, Holistic

## 5. Brand Values

### 1. Craft and Quality
Every website is built for the specific business it represents. The dentist's site should feel like a dental clinic: clean, trustworthy, easy to navigate. The plumber's site should feel like a reliable trade: clear service area, direct contact, visible reviews. No templates. Every layout, every line of copy, every image choice is made for this client.

### 2. Radical Transparency
We show prices before anyone asks. We explain what is included and what is not. We tell clients how long it takes. If something will not work for their business, we say so — even if it means losing the deal. Pricing on the website, in proposals, and in the first conversation. No hidden fees.

### 3. Accessibility
Professional web design should not require a $5,000 budget. A plumber with a great reputation and twenty five-star reviews deserves the same quality online presence as the corporate chain down the street. $299 for a five-page website. $149 for a landing page. $52 per month for hosting. Set to undercut every professional alternative in the market.

### 4. Results Over Aesthetics
A beautiful website that does not convert is a decoration. We design for outcomes first — calls, bookings, form submissions — then refine the aesthetics around that foundation. Every site includes a contact form, a visible phone number, and a clear call-to-action above the fold.

### 5. Speed and Reliability
Local businesses lose money every day they are offline or invisible. Fast delivery is not a marketing claim — it is a commitment to not wasting a client's time. Once a site is live, it stays live. Hosting is monitored. Problems get fixed fast. Support responds in under 24 hours.

## 6. Target Audience Personas

### Persona 1 — Maria, the Dentist

**Age:** 45 | **Location:** Mid-size city (Toronto, Buenos Aires, Manila)
**Business:** Solo dental practice, 8 years established
**Online presence:** Listed on one directory site. No website.

Maria's practice runs on referrals and walk-ins. But the dental chain that opened six blocks away has a full website with online booking and testimonials. She has started noticing new patients mention the chain. Her front desk spends hours answering questions a website FAQ would handle in seconds.

**What she wants:** New patients without a marketing degree. Something professional that will not embarrass her practice. Online booking.
**What she fears:** Getting locked into a long project with a vendor who disappears. Paying thousands for something she does not understand.
**Message for Maria:** "You have spent eight years building a reputation. Let us make it findable. Professional dental website — online booking, patient FAQs, your credentials displayed — $299."

### Persona 2 — James, the Plumber

**Age:** 38 | **Location:** Suburban market (Chicago suburbs, regional UK, outer Sydney)
**Business:** Owner-operator plumbing company, 6 staff, 12 years in operation
**Online presence:** Google Maps listing only.

James has built his business on word-of-mouth and has 37 five-star Google reviews. But competitors with worse reviews show up first in "plumber near me" searches because they have websites. He knows it is a problem but is suspicious of being overcharged.

**What he wants:** More calls. Something that looks like a real business. An easy way to show his reviews.
**What he fears:** Getting scammed by a tech vendor. Paying for something he cannot maintain.
**Message for James:** "You have 37 five-star reviews and no website to send people to. Your competitors show up in Google because they do. Five-page site with your reviews, service area map, and a booking form — $299."

### Persona 3 — Sofia, the Restaurant Owner

**Age:** 52 | **Location:** Urban neighborhood (Mexico City, Miami, London)
**Business:** Family restaurant, 18 years, 14 tables, strong regulars
**Online presence:** Active Facebook page (3,200 followers). No website.

Sofia runs a full dining room on weekends. But she has started losing private event bookings because when people search for "private dining [neighborhood]," she does not show up. A competitor that opened 18 months ago has already taken three of her regular large-group bookings.

**What she wants:** Private event inquiries. An updatable menu. Search visibility.
**What she fears:** A website that looks nothing like her restaurant. A process that takes months.
**Message for Sofia:** "A Facebook page brought you this far. A website will bring you private event bookings. Five pages — menu, gallery, reservation form, your story, contact — $299."

## 10. What Athena Studios Is Not

- Not a large agency. We build, deliver, and support. No account managers or quarterly review cycles.

## 11. Brand Application Checklist

Before publishing any Athena Studios content:

- [ ] Does it lead with the client's benefit, not our feature?
- [ ] Is the price visible if this is a sales context?
- [ ] Does it reference something specific about the client or their industry?
- [ ] Is it free of forbidden words (see Words We Never Use)?
- [ ] Does it end with a question or a clear single call to action?
- [ ] Does it avoid mentioning AI, automation, or autonomous systems?
- [ ] Would a 45-year-old dentist with no tech background understand every sentence?
