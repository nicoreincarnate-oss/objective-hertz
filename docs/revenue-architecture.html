Lead Research
titan/pipeline/lead_research.py

What Happens

  • Scrape website + Google results + social
  • 4 concurrent via semaphore
  • Quality gate: score ≥4 points or reject
  • Two-pass LLM: fast first, smart if weak or high-score
  • Extract: services, locations, trust signals

External APIs

ClawdBot A2A: web_scrape
ClawdBot A2A: browser_task
ClawdBot A2A: enrich_lead

Output

  • research_summary, lead_score (0-100), research_facts (JSON)
  • State: discovered → researched
  • Cost: $0 (Firecrawl free tier)

03
Email Composition
titan/pipeline/email_compose.py

What Happens

  • Try 4 installed skills first (cold-email, cold-outreach, etc.)
  • Fallback: Claude generates directly
  • Model: Sonnet for score ≥80, Haiku for rest
  • Prompt injected with: soul_copy + titan_rules + learnings
  • prompt_version_hash stored for causal tracing

Content Validation

  • BLOCK: fabricated client counts, fake awards
  • BLOCK: ACT NOW, LIMITED TIME, CLICK HERE
  • BLOCK: subject >80 chars, body >1000 chars

Output

  • Unique email per lead (<150 words)
  • State: researched → email_drafted
  • Cost: $0.003/email (Haiku)
▼
04
Email Send
titan/pipeline/email_send.py + compliance.py

What Happens

  • Micro-review: 3 AI personas score risk (≥70 to pass)
  • Provider bucketing: Gmail 100/d, Outlook 100/d, Other 50/d
  • Compliance gate: unsub check + HMAC link + footer + audit log
  • Send via Instantly.ai campaign
  • Immutable outbound_email_log (audit trail)

External APIs

Instantly POST /campaigns/{id}/leads
Instantly GET /campaigns/{id}/analytics

Safety Gates

  • Unsubscribed leads blocked
  • Dedup: already-sent emails skipped
  • Warmup ramp enforced (10→600 over 21 days)
  • State: email_drafted → email_sent
  • Cost: $97/mo Instantly subscription
▼
05
Follow-Up & Reply Handling
titan/pipeline/follow_up.py

Reply Classification

  • Layer 1: Deterministic unsub scan (11 languages)
  • Layer 2: LLM classifies: interested / not_interested / question / unsubscribe / OOO
  • Causal attribution: which sentence triggered the reply?

Follow-Up Rules

  • Max 7 follow-ups per lead
  • Min 3 days between contacts
  • First 3: always send
  • After 3: AI decides if worth continuing
  • Each follow-up: different angle, <80 words
  • State: email_sent → interested / replied / lost
  • Cost: $0 (included in Instantly)

08
Invoice + Payment Collection
titan/pipeline/invoice.py + tools/payment_router.py

Invoice Creation

  • Route: Stripe (primary) → Wise (fallback) → Conway USDC
  • Stripe: POST /v1/payment_links with idempotency key
  • Wise: POST /v1/quotes + /v1/transfers
  • Conway: x402 USDC on Base L2
  • Dedup: SHA-256(email+amount+currency) prevents double-invoice

Payment Verification

  • Stripe: poll payment_intents for status=succeeded
  • On payment: deals.status → paid, clients.status → paid
  • All prior emails marked as positive training examples
  • sales_completed counter incremented
  • If sales ≥ 10: AUTONOMY UNLOCKED
  • State: deployed → invoiced → paid
  • Processing: 2.9% + $0.30 (Stripe)

Safety Gates & Compliance
Research Quality Gate
lead_research.py
Score ≥4 points from scrape quality metrics. Placeholder text, nav-only content, and boilerplate detected and rejected.
Content Validation
email_compose.py
Rejects fabricated claims (fake client counts, awards, media mentions), spam triggers (ACT NOW, CLICK HERE), and length violations.
Micro-Review Simulation
email_send.py
3 AI personas (skeptical owner, time-starved owner, deliverability guard) must score ≥70. Below 70 = email not sent.
Compliance Chokepoint
compliance.py
ALL outbound email goes through send_to_instantly(). HMAC-signed unsub link, physical address footer, immutable audit log. Dedup prevents double-send on restart.
Demo/Site QA Gate
close_deal.py + build_site.py
Visual critic (Playwright screenshots + Claude vision) must pass before proposal or deployment. Fail-closed by default.
Review Mode (First 10)
close_deal.py + review_mode.py
First 10 sales require operator approval via Telegram (/approve). After 10: autonomy_unlocked event, review_mode disabled.
Multilingual Unsub Detection
follow_up.py
Deterministic keyword scan in 11 languages (EN, ES, FR, DE, PT, IT, NL, JA, KO, ZH) BEFORE LLM classification. Legal safety net.
Revenue Expansion ROI Gate
expansion.py
New capabilities only adopted if: revenue_gain > cost, ROI ≥1.5x, shadow rollout beats baseline. Prevents spending on unproven tools.
Lead State Machine
discovered → researched → email_drafted → email_sent → replied / interested → demo_built → proposal_sent → negotiating → closed → building → deployed → invoiced → paid
Terminal states: paid (success) · lost (any stage) · unsubscribed (legal, permanent)
Retry: unresponsive → email_drafted (after 30 days)
