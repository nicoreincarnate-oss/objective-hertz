# Launch Roadmap

## Phase 1: Legal Blockers + Warmup Start (Days 1-7)
**Goal**: Fix all P0 blockers. Begin email warmup. Start Mac Studio setup.
**Success**: Compliance gate passes. Warmup running. DNS authenticated.

## Phase 2: Infrastructure + Lead Pipeline (Days 7-14)
**Goal**: Stable Mac Studio. Email templates ready. 100+ pre-qualified leads.
**Success**: All daemons stable 48hrs. Sales playbook written. Lead backlog built.

## Phase 3: Shadow Mode Dry Run (Days 14-19)
**Goal**: Full pipeline end-to-end with real data, no external sends.
**Success**: 100+ leads processed. Zero CRITICAL errors. War Room review flow smooth.

## Phase 4: Soft Launch — First Emails (Days 19-28)
**Goal**: First real cold emails with full manual oversight.
**Success**: 100+ emails sent. Open rate >40%. 5+ replies. Zero spam complaints.

## Phase 5: First Revenue (Days 28-42)
**Goal**: Convert prospects to paying customers.
**Success**: First paying customer. 3+ invoices sent. Conversion funnel documented.

## Phase 6: Semi-Automation (Days 42-60)
**Goal**: Automate proven parts. Expand to second city.
**Success**: 10+ customers. $3K MRR. <2hr/day operator time.

## Phase 7: Scale (Days 60-75)
**Goal**: 3-5 cities. Full automation. MAGMA learning active.
**Success**: 30+ customers. $8K MRR. <1hr/day oversight.
