# General Home Services Outreach — Prompt Template
# Loaded by: titan/pipeline/email_compose.py
# Industry: HVAC, landscaping, roofing, pest control, cleaning, painting, fencing, handyman

## Personalization Hooks (use from research_facts)

- Google Maps reviews and rating
- Seasonal relevance: HVAC peaks summer/winter, landscaping spring/fall, roofing after storms
- Service photos from social media or Google Business Profile
- Service area and neighborhoods
- Owner name (first name only)
- Years in business or "serving [City] since [year]"

## Pain Point (pick ONE that fits)

- "Your best marketing is your reputation — a website makes it findable."
- "When [City] homeowners need [service], they search online first. Are you showing up?"
- "You have great reviews but nowhere to send potential customers."
- "A Facebook page isn't enough — customers expect a real website."

## Value Proposition

- Affordable professional website that shows up in local search
- Before/after photo gallery for their work
- Service area coverage map
- Customer review showcase
- Online quote request or booking form
- Under $325 one-time, live in 48 hours

## Tone

Warm and practical. Home services customers range from blue-collar trades to small business owners. Be friendly, not corporate. Show you understand their work by referencing seasonal patterns or specific services.

## Subject Line Variants (A/B test)

- A: "Quick question about [Business Name]'s online presence"
- B: "[First Name], your [Industry] business deserves more than a Facebook page"

## Industry-Specific Hooks

- **HVAC**: "Summer is coming — homeowners are already searching for AC repair in [City]"
- **Landscaping**: "Spring is the busiest season — is your website ready for the leads?"
- **Roofing**: "After every storm, homeowners search 'roofer near me' — will they find you?"
- **Pest control**: "Nobody wants to call a random number for pest control — they want to see a real business"
- **Cleaning**: "Residential cleaning is trust-based — a professional website builds that trust instantly"

## Language

Default English. Switch to Spanish if research indicates Spanish-speaking market or bilingual business.

## Constraints

- Under 150 words
- No bait subject lines
- Reference at least ONE specific fact about their business or industry
- Use seasonal timing if applicable (current month/season)
- End with a question, not a statement
- Never promise specific results (rankings, leads) — promise professional presence
