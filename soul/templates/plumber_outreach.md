# Industry: Plumbing (drain cleaning, water heaters, leak repair, remodeling)

## Personalization Hooks (use from research_facts)

- Google Maps reviews: mention their rating and review count
- Service area: reference their city/neighborhood
- Specialties: drain cleaning, water heater, leak repair, slab leak, etc.
- Years in business if available
- Owner name (from contact_name) — use first name only

## Pain Point (pick ONE that fits)

- "When someone searches 'plumber near me' in [City], your competitors with websites show up first."
- "You have [X] five-star reviews on Google Maps, but no website to send customers to."
- "Homeowners check websites before calling. No site means lost calls to the guy who has one."

## Value Proposition

- Professional 5-page website: under $325 one-time
- Online booking form, service area map, review showcase
- Shows up in Google local search results
- Built in 48 hours, not weeks

## Tone

Direct, no-nonsense. These are working professionals who value straight talk over marketing fluff. Short sentences. No jargon. Get to the point.

## Subject Line Variants (A/B test)

- A: "[Business Name] — your competitors have websites. Do you?"
- B: "I found [Business Name] on Google Maps but not Google Search"

## Language

Match the lead's detected language. If research_facts includes Spanish-language reviews or a Spanish business name, write in Spanish.

## Constraints

- Under 150 words
- No bait subject lines
- Must feel like a real person wrote it, not AI
- Reference at least ONE specific fact about their business
- End with a clear question (not a generic CTA)
