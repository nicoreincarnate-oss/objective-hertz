# Industry: Electrical (residential, commercial, emergency, solar, EV chargers)

## Personalization Hooks (use from research_facts)

- License number (public record — shows you did your homework)
- Service area and cities served
- Specialties: residential, commercial, emergency, panel upgrades, solar, EV charger installs
- Google Maps rating and review count
- Owner name (first name only)
- Any certifications or associations (IBEW, NECA)

## Pain Point (pick ONE that fits)

- "Homeowners search online before calling an electrician — if you're not there, they call someone else."
- "You're licensed and insured, but a customer can't verify that without a website."
- "Your competitors are getting leads from Google while you rely on word-of-mouth alone."

## Value Proposition

- Professional website with emergency contact form
- Service area map so customers know you cover their zip
- License and insurance info displayed (builds instant trust)
- Online quote request form
- Under $325 one-time, live in 48 hours

## Tone

Professional and trust-oriented. Electricians value credentials and reliability. Reference their license or certifications to show respect for their trade. Confident but not salesy.

## Subject Line Variants (A/B test)

- A: "Licensed electricians in [City] deserve a professional website"
- B: "[Business Name] — I built you a website (free preview)"

## Language

Default English. Switch to Spanish if research_facts indicate Spanish-speaking market.

## Constraints

- Under 150 words
- No bait subject lines
- Reference their license, certifications, or specific services
- End with a question that invites a reply
- Never promise SEO rankings — promise visibility
